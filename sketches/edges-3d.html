What to look at

  1. Halfway up. Every line meets the middle of a block's side, at mid-height.
  2. Climb outside the tray. Client → Pay API runs flat at the Client's height, then goes straight up a short way before the tray's edge.
  3. One step per line. Pay API → Fraud Check climbs once, just outside the Risk tray, straight to Fraud Check's mid-height.
  4. Two trays deep. Fraud Check → Ledger stays high over both rims and drops once, outside the outer tray.
  5. Same tray. Fraud Check → Review crosses no rim, so it drops a short way out from the taller block.
  6. Crossings: None, Bridge, Tunnel or Portal. None (the default) lets the lines cross as today. Otherwise, where Client → Audit crosses Notifier → Pay API it hops over (Bridge) or dives into the ground through a hole and comes back up (Tunnel). In Flat, the tunnel shows as a break in the line with a hole at each end. Portal: it goes into an upright ring and comes out of a second one past the crossing. Like the game: look into one and you see out of the other, with the line carrying on; from behind (between them) they are opaque. From above the line stops at a thin bar.
  7. Into a subgraph. Ledger → Payments drops to meet the tray's edge halfway up its rim.
  8. Flat. Switch to Flat: steps and bridges vanish, and the picture matches Today.

Sizes are rough: the step's distance from the tray, the bridge's height and width. Which line goes over is fixed (the one running left–right).
