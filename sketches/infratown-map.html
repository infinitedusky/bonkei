Sketch: InfraTown Map (made-up diagram) Zones are thin coloured floors with the name in big letters on the ground. Nodes are modelled icons with ink outlines, one per shape: box = server, rounded = service, cylinder = storage, diamond = decision. The labels are callout cards on dotted leaders. Lines are navy with an arrow partway along. Long links arc overhead, dashed.
  • Turn the view: the icons turn with the scene, because they are shapes, not pictures.
