The bonkei theme

One diagram, three looks. Classic is today. Bonkei recolours it from the prints: paper ground, ink outlines, flat washes, patterned tray sides. Bonkei · landscape goes further: each node becomes a landscape form (house, bonsai, rock, peak) on the same footprint, so lines route exactly as before.

What the theme is made of

Ground

  • Washi cream, with a grain and a soft darker edge
  • No grid: the paper is the ground
  • Shadows off; ink does the work

Line

  • Sumi ink outline on every block and tray
  • Edges in ink, a step where they climb onto a tray
  • Flat washes: tops lightest, sides one or two steps darker

Labels

  • Nodes: cream cartouche, ink border, mincho serif
  • Trays: dark indigo cartouche, like the prints' titles
  • Red seal reserved for errors and the logo

Tray sides: one pattern per depth

The bowl's side carries the pattern; the top stays plain sand so lines and labels read. Drawn in code, so any size and no image files.
